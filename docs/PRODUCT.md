# PRODUCT.md — nadamas.app

register: brand

## Product purpose

Premium swimming marketplace. Coaches publish schedules, prices, private/group
classes, availability. Swimmers discover coaches, book classes, pay in-app,
track training and progress.

**MVP focus (do not over-scope the narrative):** *find and book a swim coach
easily.* The landing exists to validate one question: would people book swim
coaches from an app?

## Users

1. **Swimmers / learners** — want to learn, improve technique, train triathlon
   / open water, find a trustworthy coach, book in minutes. Primary CTA:
   **"Encontrar coach"**.
2. **Coaches / instructors** — want students, to professionalize their business,
   automatic payments, schedule + roster management. Secondary CTA:
   **"Publicar mi perfil"**.

## Brand feeling

Premium + trust + calm + performance + community + wellbeing + water.
A blend of **Airbnb + ClassPass + a premium swimming brand**. Minimalist,
elegant, technological, premium, aquatic.

Name "nadamas" = double meaning: *nadar* (to swim) + *nada más* ("this is all
you need").

## Anti-references (must NOT feel like)

- Cheap gym
- Generic fitness app
- Kids' swim school
- Hardcore-athlete / "beast mode"
- Corporate/bootstrap template, generic SaaS-cream

## Anti-reference for craft (the real risk)

Swimming/wellness category reflex = "white + teal + wave shapes + soft
gradients". That is the first-order AI-slop reflex and this brief asks for
exactly those ingredients. Differentiation is mandatory: editorial typography,
asymmetric/varied layout rhythm, real photographic depth, restrained gradient
use (backdrop/glow, never text), and a distinctive water-flow scroll motion.
If it could be guessed as "swimming app → aqua + waves", it has failed.

## Strategic principles

- Two audiences, one page. Swimmer is primary; coach is a strong secondary
  narrative section, not a competing hero.
- Conversion over decoration. Every section moves toward "find a coach" or
  "become a coach".
- The product shown is partly aspirational (marketplace). Acceptable: the
  landing validates demand. Keep claims honest, copy concrete.

## Product pillars (core differentiators, must surface in copy)

1. **Portable progress.** Your trainings, preferences, and full history
   belong to you and travel with you. You decide whether to share them, and
   exactly what, with your next coach. You never start from zero with a new
   instructor. (Swimmer trust + retention angle. Surface in Features and the
   swimmer narrative.)
2. **Vetted, transparent coaches.** Coaches are evaluated both practically
   and theoretically. Each profile shows a clear description of their skills
   and qualities so you know what kind of professional you are booking. You
   can also rate coaches and read other swimmers' evaluations of a coach.
   (Trust/quality angle. Surface in Marketplace preview, Features, and the
   trust/social-proof framing. Honest: "evaluados" = vetted, not fake
   metrics.)
